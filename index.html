I am a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind Robotics team. Previously, I completed my postdoctoral research at Stanford with Shuran Song and Dorsa Sadigh, completed my Ph.D. at MIT advised by Antonio Torralba. I was also a founding member of Voyage AI, a language model startup acquired by MongoDB. My research focus on robotics and generative modeling.
